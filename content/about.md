## // PROFILE

> [!abstract] Mission Statement
> I find security gaps in satellite communication protocols that nobody has checked yet. Legacy satellite systems were built with almost no security — and the new systems replacing them haven't been independently verified either. I build tools to make RF security research accessible, and I break things to figure out how to protect them.

**Currently focused on:** Independent security analysis of unstudied Iridium satellite protocols — determining whether the systems that military, 5G networks, and critical infrastructure rely on are actually as secure as claimed.

## // OPERATIONS

> [!success] FEATURED — OpenVSA (Open Virtual Satellite Antenna)
> **Problem:** RF security research requires expensive satellite ground station hardware. Most researchers and students never get hands-on experience with satellite signals because the barrier to entry is too high. No tool existed to virtualize this.
>
> **What I built:** A desktop application that virtualizes satellite ground station antennas — GPredict integration, 3D antenna visualization, IQ signal file analysis, and multi-antenna beam pattern simulation. First of its kind.
>
> **Outcome:** Demonstrating at DEF CON 2026 Aerospace Village in collaboration with a major Japanese aerospace company. Core software of the demonstration ecosystem.

> [!example] Mr.Radio (mrradio.kr)
> **Problem:** RF hacking is one of the hardest security disciplines to learn because it requires physical hardware, antennas, and real signals. Existing CTF platforms don't offer virtualized RF environments — you either have the hardware or you can't practice.
>
> **What I built:** An RF hacking wargame platform with fully virtualized satellite signal environments — receive, demodulate, and decode satellite signals through a browser. Includes antenna selection and direction adjustment.
>
> **Outcome:** The only RF CTF platform offering this level of signal virtualization. Part of the DEF CON 2026 Aerospace Village demonstration.
>
> **Link:** [mrradio.kr](/project/mr_radio)

> [!example] Hack-the-Drone RF Challenges
> **Problem:** Drone hacking CTFs existed, but lacked realistic RF-layer challenges. Most drone security competitions focus on firmware or network layers, not the radio link.
>
> **What I built:** Designed and authored all RF-domain challenges for the Hack-the-Drone CTF competition in 2023 and 2024 — two consecutive years as sole RF challenge designer.
>
> **Outcome:** Participants gained hands-on experience with real RF exploitation techniques in a competitive environment.
