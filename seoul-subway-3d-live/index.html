About the simulated trains

The moving trains are invented, not live positions or arrival predictions. A single afternoon sample of Seoul TOPIS report counts from October 14, 2026 helps set a rough scale for 19 routes. Other hours and five additional routes use illustrative assumptions. This does not represent a typical timetable.

0 simulated trains on visible routes. The regional and intercity layers show static tracks.

Models and vertical track offsets are illustrative, not surveyed vehicles or tunnel depths. The animation runs offline without polling the position API.

Map data & credits

The Seoul network uses its bundled August 24, 2026 snapshot. The national layer maps 12 regional metro and light-rail routes and 21 selected intercity corridors. Overlapping services share tracks; this is not a complete or current service timetable.

Reference report-count sample: Seoul Metropolitan Government / TOPIS — 서울시 지하철 실시간 열차 위치정보, Korea Open Government License Type 1 (attribution). The moving 3D trains are synthetic, not those reports.

Rail and water geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Seoul network compilation: wantaekchoi / seoul-subway, commit 498fbc62e014, snapshot 2026-08-24. National rail was independently derived from OpenStreetMap geometry; Korail's route map and SRT's route list were used to check scope, not copied as artwork. South Korea outline: Natural Earth 1:10m countries, public domain. Source station metadata also uses Wikidata (CC0).
