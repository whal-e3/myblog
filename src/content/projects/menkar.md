MENKAR bundles the whole satellite system into one desktop app: **signal simulation**, **RF hardware virtualization**, **satellite virtualization**, and a live **connection to the ground station** on the [**mrradio.kr**](https://mrradio.kr) platform — so a complete satellite RF link, from a virtual spacecraft down to the ground station, runs end to end.

It's built to be as realistic as possible, with only minor tweaks to iron out the quirks a real satellite environment would otherwise introduce — close to the real thing, without the real-world friction. A personal project, built in my own time.
