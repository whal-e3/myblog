A national automotive hacking & defense competition (자동차 해킹·방어 경진대회). I competed as the four-person team **오똑핵** from Kyung Hee University, advancing from the online preliminary to the finals at EXCO Daegu in November 2024.

The competition spanned multiple categories; the most interesting to me were **CAN bus** — connecting a CAN analysis tool to a real car and driving its functions from a laptop, from unlocking the doors to running the wipers and headlights; and **RF keyless entry** — capturing the 433.92 MHz key-fob signal with a HackRF and replaying it to unlock a car door, plus jamming. The team won the **AUTOCRYPT Special Award** at the finals.
