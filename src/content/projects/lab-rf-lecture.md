An internal RF/SDR security lecture I designed and have delivered to my lab across 2024–2026.

I taught the basics (RF fundamentals, modulation, and reading signals on waterfall/constellation views), the SDR toolchain (RTL-SDR, HackRF, GNU Radio, Gqrx), and the common attack classes — eavesdropping, replay, MITM, spoofing. It finished with a hands-on lab: finding a live FM broadcast in Gqrx and building a GNU Radio flowgraph to demodulate it.
