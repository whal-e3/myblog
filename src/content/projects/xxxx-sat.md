I am the Responsible Lead Engineer (RLE) for the ground-system software of **PADO-SAT**, Kyung Hee University's CubeSat team selected as one of five in the basic category of the **7th Korean CubeSat Competition**. KARI announced the final selection on **October 22, 2026**. I am designing the Mission Operations Center (MOC) that will communicate with the satellite.
