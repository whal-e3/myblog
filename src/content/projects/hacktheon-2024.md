Competed in **HackTheon Sejong 2024**, an international collegiate cybersecurity competition (International University Students' Cyber Security Competition) hosted in Sejong, and reached the on-site **finals** in June 2024 at the Government Complex Sejong Convention Center.

It was my first CTF finals, and solving in the same room as teams from other countries was a new experience. The problem set leaned heavily on **forensics** — outside my usual pwnable / reversing / web / crypto — so much of it was picking up new techniques fast under time pressure.
