A desktop application that virtualizes satellite ground station antennas — GPredict integration, 3D antenna visualization, IQ signal file analysis, and multi-antenna beam pattern simulation. **First of its kind**, it was introduced at the **DEF CON 34 Aerospace Village** in 2026.

The barrier to entry for satellite RF security research is steep: ground station hardware costs thousands of dollars, antennas need rotators, and even getting a usable signal requires deep RF knowledge. OpenVSA virtualizes the entire stack so researchers and students can experiment without owning a single piece of physical equipment — a tool I built on my own personal time, independent of any funded project.
