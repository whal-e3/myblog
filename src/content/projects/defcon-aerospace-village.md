At the **DEF CON 34 Aerospace Village** (August 2026), I helped run a **Satellite Hacking** booth together with **GMO Cyber Security (IERAE)**. Visitors worked through a hands-on satellite attack-and-defense demo across four scenarios — eavesdropping a downlink, forging an uplink command, spoofing telemetry, and throwing a whole constellation into chaos.

The demo software embedded my **Virtual Satellite Antenna (VSA)** — the virtualized ground-station RF core — as the engine driving the satellite link, so people could tune, receive, and attack a virtual satellite live on the DEF CON floor.
